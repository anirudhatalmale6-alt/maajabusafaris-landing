Why travel with us

Planned by people who actually go there

Routes we know first-hand

Routes are built around where the wildlife actually is in your travel month, not around a fixed catalogue of departures. Season shapes the itinerary, not the other way round.

One planner, start to finish

The person who plans and prices your safari is the person you deal with throughout — you are not passed between a sales desk and an operations team.

Clear, itemised pricing

Quotes are itemised, so you can see what park fees, transfers, accommodation and guiding each cost — and what has deliberately been left out.

Reachable while you travel

You travel with a direct contact for the whole trip rather than a call centre, so plans can be changed on the ground when weather or wildlife makes it worth it.
